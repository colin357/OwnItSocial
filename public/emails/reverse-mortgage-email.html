Is Your Home Equity Working Hard Enough For You?

Millions of homeowners 62+ are sitting on significant equity — and may not realize they have powerful options that don’t require selling their home.

If you’re 62 or older and own your home, you may have access to a powerful retirement tool that most people don’t fully understand: a reverse mortgage (HECM).

A reverse mortgage can allow you to access a portion of your home equity — without selling your home, without making monthly mortgage payments, and without giving up ownership.
